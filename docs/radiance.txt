Radiance 2026, third edition of NIT Delhi's Annual Creative Fest, organized by Clairvoyance - The Photography and Film Club of NIT Delhi.
Radiance is envisioned as a 8-day creative learning festival designed to provide students with practical and experiential exposure to diverse domains of visual storytelling, including Photography, Filmmaking, Video Editing and Graphic Design. The festival will feature an inaugural session, interactive expert-led workshops, creative competitions, an inter-college photowalk, and a themed grand exhibition with multiple activities and art display, enabling students to learn through both guided sessions and hands-on application in a fun manner.
The event will bring together students, creative enthusiasts, college photography and film clubs, and industry professionals from across institutions, providing opportunities to explore Delhi's vibrant culture while applying workshop learnings through practical experiences. Online and offline competitions will be hosted Pan India on Unstop, with a prize pool of ₹25,000, offering students a wider platform to showcase their creative work while increasing the visibility of NIT Delhi and its creative community. Conducted in collaboration with industry professionals and leading creative brands, the event will provide valuable mentorship, real-world insights, and exposure to contemporary creative practices. The grand themed exhibition will further showcase student work to a wider audience through both on-ground display and our digital platforms.
