About

An 8-day creative learning festival

Radiance is envisioned as an 8-day creative learning festival designed to provide students with practical and experiential exposure to diverse domains of visual storytelling, including Photography, Filmmaking, Video Editing and Graphic Design.

Organized by Clairvoyance, the Photography and Film Club of NIT Delhi. The festival features an inaugural session, expert-led workshops, creative competitions, a photo walk and a themed grand exhibition.
